# Retained research diagnostics and explicit recovery handoff

Status: **HOLD**. This is a source consolidation of compatible components from
[`55033605dc`](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/Trillionnium-Chain/commit/55033605dc).
It does not activate a protocol, rerun a historic service campaign, or turn a
local observation into independent execution evidence. Existing admission,
proof, cancellation, and release owners remain authoritative.

## Complete reference search records

[`useful_cell_attempt_journal_v1.py`](../../../../formal/pon-nakamoto-v1/useful_cell_attempt_journal_v1.py)
creates a new journal and durably records each legal reference W1 attempt
before arithmetic starts. Successful attempts, misses, failed writes, unknown
outcomes, and incomplete crash tails remain distinguishable. The sink stops
proving on a write, flush, or fsync failure. Replay checks the complete five-tile
plan and, when supplied, recomputes all completed attempts from the original
material. The ordinary Cell relation verifier still checks the complete witness.
Hash chaining only binds a retained endpoint; it provides no external rollback
custody or authoritative measurement of the cheapest implementation.

[`grouped_delivery_cost_v1.py`](../../../../formal/pon-nakamoto-v1/experiments/grouped_delivery_cost_v1.py)
compares the two retained Python/NumPy delivery arms on a finite local burst.
It retains every planned request and late failure, performs full scalar
recomputation, and compares the loaded repository source files before and after
the observation. Those file hashes do not attest to loaded bytecode, NumPy,
native libraries, the interpreter, external demand, or native node performance.
The staged W1 runner now derives cost summaries from its original observations
without changing its cancellation, cleanup, or final acknowledgement owners.

## Original failed fixed-Host readback

[`fixed_host_service_readback_v1.py`](../../../../formal/pon-nakamoto-v1/experiments/fixed_host_service_readback_v1.py)
checks a previously derived evidence document and never launches a node.
The exact historical F0 analysis is retained as a
[test fixture](../../../../scripts/ci/fixtures/pon_fixed_host_root_f0_b5bb_20261105.json),
with its bytes pinned by the test. The failed baseline still contains zero of
six required confirmations and a denominator of 42 business packets. The
reader cannot authenticate that document or replace its original source,
binary, raw report, or Host execution. Output creation is exclusive: an
existing report or input cannot be overwritten by a new readback.

## Anchored trial reconciliation

The existing runtime trial ledger adds an explicit read-only `reconcile`
operation. It replays the selected journal and requires the separately retained
plan and a minimum acknowledged checkpoint from that same journal. It reports
the local attempt state while keeping external effects `UNKNOWN` and retry,
refund, and installation authority false. Reconciliation cannot bootstrap,
renew a deadline, or manufacture a replacement checkpoint.

## Versioned recovery-file handoff

The explicit V4 installed-ELF handoff passes two bounded externally pinned
recovery files to the already selected installed binary. The frontier and
signer trust remain outside the Agent home, run, workspace, and fleet rollback
domains. Their original paths and bytes are checked again before execution.
V2 and V3 retain their original argument lists and do not silently select V4.
The root-owned, caller-nonwritable installed-ELF ancestor policy is unchanged.

The pure readers and negative controls ran locally during consolidation.
Two actual nonroot installed-ELF controls could not qualify in this container:
its `/usr` ancestors are owned by the unprivileged account rather than root,
so the unchanged ownership policy rejects them. This failed qualification is
retained in the consolidation receipts. It requires a conforming host run;
the policy and required controls were not relaxed.
