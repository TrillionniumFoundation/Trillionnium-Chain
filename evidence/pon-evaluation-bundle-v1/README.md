## Scope and limits

The E3 bundle closes mutable baseline, partition and summary-score paths in the existing
producer/evaluator/settlement callers. It is not an authority issuer or a substitute for
Hepta's artifact, withdrawal, operation and final-use owners. Consumer receipt checking
now requires output, units and provider nonce as well as all request identities.

The environment names the filesystem and compiler/interpreter. Peak process RSS and
wall time are observations, not a throughput promise. Unmeasured VRAM, block inclusion
and client-confirmed throughput remain null. No paid model provider, private task source,
deployed key/database or service was used. Independent future-window model benefit,
ordinary Hepta destination integration, useful-work hardness, public hostile-proof
fairness, long-term DA, physical power loss and production activation remain unaccepted.

Raw logs include all executions; no vectors or earlier reports were regenerated to
change an outcome. These results do not establish that the routed candidate outperforms
the strongest deployable alternatives. Continue new-source and normal-owner work without
forcing adoption or treating model publication as a public redistribution grant.
