## What changed

The [protocol and recovery boundary](../../docs/protocol/pon-nakamoto-v1/details/HISTORY_FIRST_STARTUP_EMPTY_NAMESPACE_V1.md)
describes the new domain and the explicitly unimplemented partial-initialization
stages. The implementation adds:

- A fresh double-signed administrative closure for the sole known failed first
  Startup, with exact original intent/envelope/anchor, independent source/binary
  and preparation pins, a real death witness and an unchanged private container
  prepared before the original signature.
- A retained original CPU receipt and original predebit, plus an additional finite
  allowance covering the entire reaped process. Nonce, ID and original lifetime
  ceiling remain monotonic. Expired old run authority is not revived.
- A held parent/child/lease boundary: complete inventories, exact file identity,
  private modes, link counts, bytes and mtime/ctime, plus parsed identity,
  preparation and original journal-path bindings. No file is removed or reset.
- A dedicated exact-byte publication wrapper. Generic publication refuses this
  event; repair checks the old event at its original frame time and current
  repair authority at the present time while retaining the held namespace.
- One new mandatory real process method: failed paid Startup, original known
  settlement, actual birth-to-death guardian, fresh closure after budget expiry,
  actual publisher SIGKILL, expired-old-signature repair, idempotent retry and an
  ordinary new zero-credit process through validation, activation, Close and cold
  readback. It includes signature/pin/accounting/lease refusal cases and a second
  independently observed process whose newly added Native bytes must be preserved
  and refuse closure. These native assertions are implemented, **not locally
  executed**.

All 21 inherited names remain: 15 earlier recovery controls, four Startup controls
and the two b760 follow-up controls. The sole ignored helper is retained and
actually selected by the process cases. The exact new roster has 23 names and
requires 22 passed, zero failed and that one ignored helper. All original five
observer helper bodies are AST-identical to b760. The 600-second suite, 2-second
burst, 4-second operation allowance and four whole-parent-snapshot attempts are
unchanged.

## Remaining gates and boundaries

The new hosted workflow must compile this candidate, retain and reconstruct its
actual release ELF, verify its exact test inventory, execute the whole group and
strict Clippy, and collect complete raw terminal artifacts even on failure. A
partial pass or a result from #526/#535 cannot close this gate.

This bounded capability grants no arbitrary empty-directory adoption, Native
reset, partially initialized database recovery, pending Startup/Close recovery,
unknown process-tree qualification, torn-frame synthesis, extra lifetime ceiling,
signed multi-step reorg or general continuous History admission. Its own new
record's file-synced crash is part of the new native method; other new-domain
cuts remain unmeasured even though the underlying publication primitive retains
its older controls. Whole History P0 remains open.
