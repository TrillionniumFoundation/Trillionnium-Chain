## Existing M12 rules remain fixed

An original Task source is indivisible. Original M12 requires its policy cap
to equal the whole native Task amount and each original Task claim to reserve
that whole policy cap. A second positive claim cannot share the same Task
budget: cumulative monetary commitments would exceed the cap. Deleting the
old complete-plan source-alias refusal cannot make that design valid.

The proposed route therefore keeps one original M12 parent budget and claim
per group. A distinct V3 journal records the group's independent child intents,
receipts and windows. Original V1 Task/M12 schemas and the original complete-plan
unique-source route retain their existing meanings and refusal conditions.
The grouped route must not impersonate those original per-slot M12 claims.

No task is cancelled, settled early or assigned a fabricated Positive outcome
to free pending capacity. Native task amounts remain reserved and all original
monetary/resource holds remain charged. Grouping grants no divisible native
payment primitive or payment eligibility for future retention.

## Child persistence and recovery contract

The V3 journal must durably bind the original parent policy/claim/effect,
complete plan and catalog pins, provider, canonical child roster, sums and
child windows before their first physical effect. Cold open must validate its
namespace, owner/context, complete retained history and exact record identities;
it must not reconstruct expected policy from untrusted current contents or
start an empty replacement journal on a mismatch.

A child may perform its original physical write only after its first durable
New intent. Pending and Done retries reconcile the exact original target;
they do not redispatch an initial copy or mint replacement identities. Original
failed attempts, cost debits and unknown results remain in history. A separate
repair group supplies fresh authority for damaged original targets.

An M12 parent is completed only after every declared child is genuinely Done
and the entire group's current original targets have been read back. A page
receipt, parent digest, in-memory map or earlier child's success cannot replace
that complete current observation. Parent Done also does not erase the child
journal or authorise another initial attempt after cold restart. Complete
release observation still requires all pages, all children, all parents and
the original version/catalog pins.
