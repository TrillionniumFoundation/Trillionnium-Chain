# Architecture boundaries and validated business observations

Status: **research candidate; HOLD / NO-GO**. This contract continues the
composed #821 source. It grants no activation or new mining credit. The sole
development plan remains the [native blockchain development plan](../../../development/TRNM_AI_NATIVE_BLOCKCHAIN_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md).

## Preserved owners and qualification boundaries

Hepta's shared base, low-rank adapters, sparse routing and private local state
remain application architecture. Task, funding, evaluation and FinalUse retain
their original owners. No Cell manifest, model improvement, cache saving or
successful application request automatically becomes consensus work.

Pure PoN retains complete W1 neural work and its original cost and resource
contracts. The existing [fixed-probability cost contract](FIXED_PROBABILITY_COST_CONTRACT_V1.md)
and [classical/quantum applicability audit](CLASSICAL_QUANTUM_COST_AUDIT_20261108.md)
keep minimum legal costs conditional on their declared hypotheses. A finite
credit solver, implementation operation count or one successful ticket is not
an all-algorithm lower bound. Shared preparation, failures, restarts, hidden
progress and retained private state belong to the complete lifecycle.

For each Cell family, application validity, model quality and work qualification
are separate claims. A future positive work qualification must bind its exact
relation, public sampling and target, permitted preparation and state, complete
verification, minimum legal classical/quantum cost hypotheses and a compatible
common-credit schedule. Families lacking those arguments remain unqualified;
they do not borrow eligibility from another family or from complete W1.

## One authentication responsibility across close paths

The ordinary protected listener and remote History share the original mutation
CPU domain and its `ListenerIntake`. Authentication has a separate measured
ledger in that intake. A scalar CPU observation being known does not establish
that authentication accounting is known.

An authentication timing failure, checked-accounting failure or unwind can
latch that ledger unavailable. Every service/History closer must preserve the
persistent unclean epoch in that case, including a PQ listener that closes
before a classical listener and a History owner that closes last. The check
must precede the non-last-listener return; otherwise another closer can erase
the earlier unknown responsibility. The listener records the same ledger's
authentication observation before completing its epoch fence.

The implementation obtains the shared intake while holding the scalar domain
lock, releases that lock, and only then observes the authentication ledger. A
missing binding retains the existing low-level owner behavior. A poisoned or
unreadable bound ledger cannot establish a clean close. This is a refusal on
the original durable marker, not a new schema, namespace or reset allowance.

Known, measured authentication refusal is distinct from unknown accounting:
normal clean shutdown must continue to work. Complete persistence of known
authentication charges across clean restarts, continuous-owner integration,
all entrypoint resource aggregation and a justified replenishment model remain
open. This change therefore does **not** qualify the complete persistent
resource contract.

## A parent becomes extendable after complete committed validation

The retained native gate requires a committed parent state. Full W1 completion,
prepared execution and full M06 execution before durable commit do not confer
parent qualification. A fully committed valid side branch may be extended even
before it becomes the active tip. Eligibility must not be confused with active
chain selection.

Two cross-stage native controls cover both Legacy and AuthenticatedV1. They
exercise real parent and child work, builder/context/admission paths, activation,
cancellation after the persistence delta but before commit, independent SQLite
visibility, rollback, cold reopen and successful retry. No confirmed runtime
bypass was found in this slice; the change adds direct regression evidence for
the existing rule.

## Validated business goodput

The new [reader](../../../../scripts/read_validated_business_goodput.py) observes
the retained original campaign without replacing its v1/v2/v3 readers or gates:

\[
G = \frac{\text{unique business satisfying full validation and confirmation}}{\text{actual elapsed time}}.
\]

In this campaign, business identity is the signed transaction ID. Six empty
maintenance successors provide the installed confirmation depth and work; they
are not six additional business items. Reset phases and repeated experiments
replay the same business and are reported separately.

The observation joins the original manifest and source receipts, exact packet
bytes, transaction commitment, successful native Work/History import receipts,
and the installed depth-six/required-work-six confirmation receipt. Complete
Work must precede the start of the **same** qualifying confirmation call. A
later Work observation cannot retroactively validate an earlier confirmation.
Equal block IDs with differing complete packet bytes are rejected, including
historical imports without a Work observer. Negative confirmation objects also
need an actual native confirmation receipt.

The original three attempt slots, three phases, three scheduled rounds per
phase and 30-second pressure window remain unchanged. The numerator includes
qualifying business confirmed within that original window; the denominator is
the whole actual parent-phase elapsed time, including overrun. It is a bounded
campaign observation, not general steady-state or WAN throughput. The fixed
window is also reported explicitly.

Missing Work/confirmation observations, unfinished scheduled rounds or missing
elapsed time produce `null` definitive goodput. The separately labelled
observed numerator may be zero while the true rate is unknown. An original
nonzero exit or failed campaign remains a failure. Successful-round p95/p99 are
withheld below 20/100 samples and never stand in for an honest-task tail claim.
Whole lifecycle cost and abandoned producer work remain unknown when not
observed. Application savings and model benefit are separate unmeasured fields.

The reader binds recorded observations and byte identities. It does not execute
W1, reverify signatures, attest the host, authenticate finality, prove a cost
lower bound or authorize execution. Its adversarial controls are synthetic
reader tests, not a new native campaign. The [evidence record](../../../../evidence/chain-architecture-boundaries-20261109/README.md)
separates those controls from actual native execution and retained-log readback.

## Composition and remaining gates

The closed readiness source parser recognizes two explicitly reviewed
`SelectedOpen` layouts. It chooses one from the enum's code tokens, checks that
layout's complete bindings and exact implementation hash, and fails on any
mismatch. It does not retry a historical profile after failure. Both profiles
return only the existing legacy DDL scope; authenticated, growth and PQ storage
are not promoted into that historical readiness claim.

DAGs, parallel chains, recursion and grouped GEMM may change implementation
costs. Their gains need fresh observations under the boundaries above, including
honest-task tails, complete cost and stale work. Independent signature,
KEM/TLS, hash commitment, Fiat-Shamir/QROM, migration and parameter-composition
reviews remain necessary for a whole-chain quantum-resistance claim.

Original Host/resource and native campaigns remain required on their original
profiles. A local diagnostic build with fewer compiler jobs or debug symbols
does not satisfy those campaigns. No historical passing branch, newly passing
reader or fixed source contract upgrades a failed/incomplete composed run.
