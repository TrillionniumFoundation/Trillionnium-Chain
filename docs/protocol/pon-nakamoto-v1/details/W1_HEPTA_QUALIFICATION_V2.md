## Controlled mechanics and beneficial releases are separate results

The current [end-to-end learning contract, section 8](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/blob/de133b601e133917cbe1ae03efb3a2ffc06dcd4a/docs/lane-e/END_TO_END_LEARNING_SEQUENCE.md)
explicitly permits public or previously seen tasks in the frozen engineering
evaluation. Unseen future data and independent data custody are not entry
conditions for the controlled three-round mechanism test. This corrects any
broader claim that controlled development must wait for a future holdout.
Original evaluator/selector credentials, installed-parent readback, declared
exposure/reuse, lifecycle cost and evaluation controls remain mandatory.

A no-update cycle can establish correct training, evaluation, refusal,
reconciliation and continued ordinary use of the old authorized parent. The
unexecuted candidate settlement/fetch/install stages must be explicitly marked
not applicable with the genuine decision reason in a versioned record.
Three no-update cycles cannot establish three beneficial child releases or the
separate successful cross-node fetch/install/next-task cohort.

Beneficial releases require distinct accepted artifacts that exceed their actual
installed parents under the frozen threshold/confidence rules while passing
retention, safety and resource checks. Longitudinal efficacy retains the
two-calendar-window obligation. A reused engineering dataset cannot also be
reported as previously unseen future evidence.

## What current evidence does and does not establish

The retained [real-model scope](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/blob/de133b601e133917cbe1ae03efb3a2ffc06dcd4a/qualification/learning-generations/real-model-owner-controlled-20261025/SCOPE.md)
contains actual model parameter generations, artifact publication/recovery,
signed read/unload and local comparison observations on their named historical
sources. It also retains the original timeout and broken-pipe failures. These
are substantive component observations, and none is re-executed or relabeled by
this source review. Direct model forwards are not automatically CURRENT-bound
ordinary product tasks. Fetching backbone weights plus a separate adapter is
not automatically a selected complete Cell release closure.

O06 therefore still needs a real authorized initial neural parent and one
joined ordinary-task lineage through the existing owners, followed by the
three actual controlled cycles. An eligible-update cycle must include exact
Chain observation, complete cross-node release retrieval, permitted install,
later ordinary-task readback and recovery/rollback evidence. Product mechanism,
successful transfer/install, beneficial releases and public independent
operation must each retain their own verdict. Source completeness or training
expenditure cannot replace the missing outcome.
