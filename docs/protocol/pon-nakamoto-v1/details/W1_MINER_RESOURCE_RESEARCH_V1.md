# W1 phase-separated miner resource experiment

Status: research-only native experiment. It preserves the existing W1 relation,
native Node, canonical packet, target, chainwork and qualification flags. This
continues the [cost game](W1_COST_SECURITY_GAME.md) and the
[sole development plan](../../../development/TRNM_AI_NATIVE_BLOCKCHAIN_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md).
The previous [complete-budget experiment](W1_ADAPTIVE_JOINT_RESEARCH.md#7-equal-budget-native-admission-continuation)
retains its original meaning: its strategy interval includes receiving and
verification observers. This new namespace measures a different endpoint.

## 1. Physically separate the four phases

The new [native executable](../../../../trillionnium/crates/trnm-pon-node/examples/pon_w1_miner_budget_research.rs)
and [research implementation](../../../../trillionnium/crates/trnm-pon-node/examples/support/w1_miner_budget.rs)
execute exactly one ordered sequence:

| Phase | Actual work included | Permitted interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup `P` | Original Node and task source; required signed zero-task registration; paid native template search/admission; actual ancestor advancement; selected hot preparation; retained setup records | Observed cost of this chosen acquisition/preprocessing policy, including its native setup observers |
| Miner `B` | Paid task/parent/seed selection; noise screening where selected; cold preparation where selected; complete original proof; ticket calculation; full encoding/output flush and retained proof material | Cost of this implemented producer until complete bytes are available |
| Receiver | Original Node admission of every generated attempt, including target misses, after miner completion; native owner teardown | Native contextual accept/reject result, with no selection feedback to the miner |
| Audit | Both native full relation verifiers; reconstruction of original setup search misses; all retained owned-material teardown | Posthoc validation, not additional mining work or credited output |

The miner function has no Node, Context, verifier or audit callback. Receiver
and audit do not interleave with it. Their durations are measured after the
miner boundary; the analysis does not subtract guessed observer costs from an
interleaved strategy interval. The whole-command CPU observation additionally
retains startup and final encoding/teardown residuals. Individual stage charges
are nested within phase totals and must not be added twice.

`P` and `B` are separate process-CPU nanosecond limits. Setup must complete within
`P` before mining may start. Each native operation is cooperative and may finish
after its phase limit. Every overshoot remains in actual phase cost; full proof
bytes flushed after `B` receive no within-`B` credit. A later receiver success
does not change that timestamp. Conversely, a proof available within `B` that is
subsequently accepted is not described as admission completed within `B`.

The availability stamp follows serialization and `stdout.flush()` of the whole
proof record. This is local output availability, not acknowledged network
delivery or durable remote storage. Wall and CPU clocks remain separate.
`hot`/`cold` mean arithmetic preparation policies, not hardware-cache flushing.
Proof and logging costs are part of this implementation, not a theorem that an
optimal miner must use the same diagnostic encoding or output sink.

## 2. Paid tasks, historical ancestors and algorithms

The experiment uses two native development materials: maintenance and a signed
zero-product task. Public task provenance, real demand and model quality are not
created by the disclosed development signers.

`selective` acquires only the task alternatives used by the selected policy;
`shared` acquires both. Maintenance-only selective acquisition therefore avoids
the unnecessary zero-task registration. Other acquisition policies can change
the actual parent history. The analysis retains those differences rather than
forcing a paired comparison.

`parents=1..4` constructs an actual ancestor bank on one paid native path. Work
can select a historical parent from that bank. This is a finite multi-parent
opportunity experiment, not an arbitrary concurrent fork forest, distributed
publication race or perpetual cache lifetime. A historical parent's local
admission does not imply current-tip growth, canonical inclusion or a matured
reward. Research candidates are not activated as the next tip.

Fixed task policies are maintenance-only and zero-only. Round-robin cycles
eligible tasks and, when configured, their ancestor bank. Paid-noise screening
computes and records every declared candidate's noise score, chooses a stable
minimum, then generates a complete transcript. With parent policy round-robin,
this screening policy considers the entire finite bank; it is not sequential
single-parent screening. The noise is regenerated by the producer and this
extra cost is charged. Screening does not observe a ticket or future receiver
result. It is one declared adaptive policy, not an optimal selector.

| Declared arm | Maintenance implementation | Zero-task implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prepared-generic` | Existing generic prepared producer | Existing generic prepared producer |
| `integer-paired-general` | Existing general integer-paired producer | Existing general integer-paired producer |
| `structure-specialized` | Existing maintenance limb producer | Existing blocked-zero producer |
| `structure-paired` | Existing maintenance limb producer | Existing blocked-zero integer-paired producer |

Unsupported structure is an error, not a silent fallback. Every admitted result
must retain exact original W1 semantics; a source-level operation count does not
declare a timing winner. Earlier AVX2 joint experiments remain separate evidence;
this experiment does not claim a new batch implementation or GPU execution.

## 3. Frozen campaign and comparison rules

The [registered plan](../../../../formal/pon-nakamoto-v1/experiments/w1_miner_resource_campaign_v1.json)
contains 58 jobs, frozen with its readers and analysis before performance:

- 32 fixed-task jobs: two seeds, two tasks, hot/cold and four arms. Arm order is
  reversed within each task/cache cell on the second seed.
- 16 bank jobs: two seeds, round-robin or paid screening, one/four ancestors,
  and general paired versus structure-paired. The second seed reverses arm order.
- Four shared-acquisition jobs have selective counterparts in the fixed cohort.
- Six controls retain zero/tight setup, zero/tight miner budget and hot/cold caps.

Normal jobs declare `P=1,000,000,000 ns`, `B=128,000,000 ns` and a 4,096-attempt
safety cap. A cap reached before full budget observation is censored; it is not
the maximum budget yield. All completed misses, later winners and unfinished
selections remain in the denominator and raw record. No failed job is replaced
by a favorable seed, task, target or enlarged budget.

The registered reference arm is `integer-paired-general`. Comparisons retain
every other registered arm against that reference and hot against cold. Paired
ratios require complete uncensored observations, the same relevant options,
the entire identical paid acquisition and template bank, and identical
overlapping semantic challenge/proof histories. Divergent acquisition histories
are reported side by side; they do not receive an identity-matched ratio.
Zero denominators stay null. These are descriptive finite observations, not
confidence bounds on the fastest possible algorithm or a selected production
`kappa`.

Report the nominal `B`, actual setup and miner CPU, their sum, receiver CPU,
audit CPU, signed whole-child residual, wall duration and whole-command peak RSS
separately. A rate conditioned on an existing preprocessing state is distinct
from a rate including its observed acquisition cost. Token fees are not added
to CPU nanoseconds. A setup tail may admit a registration candidate and stop
before activating it: its committed candidate fee receipt remains retained,
while the separately reported active economy still belongs to the old tip.
Those candidate fee units are not an active-chain debit or a matured payment.
Whole-command RSS is not a miner-only memory peak. No energy,
physical bandwidth or network throughput is inferred from these counters.

## 4. Full proof and source replay

The [collector](../../../../scripts/run_w1_resource_research.py) requires clean,
exact committed source, a committed plan, the locked Cargo graph and a fresh
release target. It retains actual GNU time and waited-child observations,
commands, original receipts, failures, stdout and stderr. The
[bundle reader](../../../../scripts/read_w1_resource_research.py) requires
external head/tree/plan/binary/manifest pins and checks the complete transitive
reader source/data closure, both phase boundaries and independent full-proof
arithmetic replay.

Original Node setup searches do not expose their losing proof bytes during
search. Their original paid search count is retained, and their full misses are
reconstructed only in audit. Repeated setup winners and reconstructed misses
are counted as replay records, never new opportunities. Known setup winning
identities remain separate from fresh miner output.

This evidence can close a measurement-scope defect and falsify an overly strong
reference-cost claim. It cannot prove actual SHA-256 instantiation, arbitrary
multi-instance hardness, hardware independence, honest network growth or Hepta
efficacy. Those remain governed by the
[resource reduction](W1_RESOURCE_REDUCTION_V1.md),
[chain envelope](W1_CHAIN_SECURITY_ENVELOPE_V1.md) and
[external qualification boundaries](W1_EXTERNAL_QUALIFICATION_V1.md).
