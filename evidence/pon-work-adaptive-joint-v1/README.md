## Findings

Long-budget hot AVX2/sequential inclusive opportunity-rate ratios are 1.140313
(full-rank field), 1.462808 (zero), and 1.101103 (maintenance). Short-budget results
include 0.833662; the near-q width-two arithmetic control is 0.317594.
These are finite single-host software observations, not a universal bound.

All six parent pairs have identical within-budget winner counts and work histories.
They stop at 32 research attempts before spending the 1-second budget. Their
0.989837–1.234172 consumed-CPU rate ratios are not increases in maximum budget yield.
Paid popcount screening has no demonstrated stable throughput advantage.

controls, 34 source-integrity controls, 91 CI contract controls, 200 Rust test

## Reproduction and limits

Use the [frozen research contract](../../docs/protocol/pon-nakamoto-v1/details/W1_ADAPTIVE_JOINT_RESEARCH.md)
and [registered plan](../../formal/pon-nakamoto-v1/experiments/w1_adaptive_joint_campaign_v1.json).
The exact externally pinned replay command is in validation.json. Extract the core
campaign directory and keep its original metadata; the reader supports relocated
raw files. Replay in a clean checkout whose scripts, formal and config bytes match
the measured commit, with bytecode generation disabled. Pins must come from the
trusted owner/publication, not solely from an untrusted bundle.

Joint is relation-only and counts complete proofs by its producer budget. Parent
counts original admissions by its whole-strategy budget. Their denominators stay
separate. Clocks, native eligibility/admission and RSS are software observations.
No measured joules, real transfer, independent GPU/ARM/ASIC, new Hepta training,
production activation or cheapest-miner qualification is supplied.
