This research crate's src/sha_schedule_air.rs adapts portions of Plonky3
p3-sha256-air version 0.8.0, published with license MIT OR Apache-2.0.
Upstream: https://github.com/Plonky3/Plonky3
The Apache-2.0 option is used for this adaptation; its license is included
in LICENSE-PLONKY3-APACHE-2.0.

Changes: the canonical public message schedule is independently reconstructed
into preprocessing, corresponding witness columns and constraints are omitted,
and the remaining official compression column layout and formulas are adapted
into the existing complete-public-W1 IV/chaining/output wrapper.
Exact upstream source hashes and the relation argument are recorded in
PUBLIC_SCHEDULE_BACKEND.md.
