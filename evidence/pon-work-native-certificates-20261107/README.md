## What the query theorem certifies

In a domain-separated ideal terminal random oracle, let `Q` count all relevant
full-value queries and `M` bound all complete outputs, including those submitted
without a prior query. Grant the adversary all unit work and legal batching for
free. A conservative simulator uses `N = 2*Q + M` Boolean queries. At most 128
research credit is available per distinct MU1 identity, so weight at least `x`
requires at least `ceil(x/128)` distinct successful terminal inputs.

The finite recording-tail derivation includes arbitrary coherent strategies;
the initial joint oracle state must either be independent or have a valid
residual-tail certificate. Self-declaring that preprocessing and public help
were charged is not evidence that a real execution met the budget. This
resource is an ideal query count, not machine seconds, gates, FLOPs or energy.
The probability parameters do not constitute an actual SHA-256 reduction.

MU1 research credit `16*u` and its new terminal-oracle identity are not the
currently deployed native `required_work(target)` and native block identity.
No adapter establishing equality of their accepted relation, identity and weight
has been proved or enabled. The MU1 tail therefore cannot be inserted directly
into the present six-successor witness by renaming both quantities `A`.
Likewise, an unconditional lifetime bound is not the conditional risk after a
randomly selected confirmation. A fixed-epoch total-work domination and a union
bound need no independence, but a restarted conditional statement needs its
own valid joint residual-state and budget certificate.

## What the execution theorem certifies

For a reference score `R`, complete eligible maximum `M` and selected score `D`,
availability deficit `(R-M)+` plus selection deficit `(min(R,M)-D)+` equals
`(R-D)+`. Paying these terms yields a deterministic confirmation/fork witness
even at an early cross-view observation. It can be vacuous when the target
block is not yet available; that is an explicit unresolved network term.

Finite declared trace validation does not authenticate all eligible competitors,
unseen branches, a global physical clock, private work or the probability of
its environmental envelope. The native source audit records that the live
owner does not export all of these objects. A schema label, source hash or
synthetic trace cannot supply the missing trust root.
