## Reproduction and integrity

Each lane documents its precise readback command and exclusions. The root
[files.json](files.json) binds all recovery bytes, excluding itself; each lane
also has a scoped manifest. Exact raw artifact members, recovered Git blobs,
new packaging and new readback processes are labelled separately. Temporary
signed download URLs are not retained.

Recovering an observed successful rejection does not prove a minimum mining
resource cost. Thousands of rotating keys from 24 processes are not thousands
of independent physical operators. The 11,772 reported native admissions are
observations across fresh stores, with 654 distinct paired packet IDs; they are
not 11,772 distinct canonical blocks or chainwork credits.

No main merge, production activation, whole-chain quantum-security qualification,
arbitrary-Sybil fairness guarantee or Hepta autonomous-upgrade acceptance is
claimed by this delivery.
