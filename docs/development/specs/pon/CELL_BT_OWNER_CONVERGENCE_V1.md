## One complete model and environment after each custody route

The shared research entry keeps three mutually exclusive typed routes:

| Route | Original input and owner |
| --- | --- |
| Complete copy | Original V2 independently pinned model/environment input and complete-copy retention |
| Paged custody | Original paged wrapper, independent expected CTS1/TCV1/TCV2/transport pins, exact slots and page profile |
| Native callback | Original V2 source and original M12 grouped parent/CGJ3 child owners |

Choosing the native callback does not synthesize the independently pinned paged
wrapper's expected context. No operation runs both custody owners, creates a
second funding ledger or silently falls back after refusal. Each callback uses
the original absolute deadline and original operation/output identity.

After retention/recovery, the common consumer path rechecks fresh CPV4 context
and every member of the complete TCV1/TCV2 closure. It then takes the manager's
original CAS ticket, selects the exact CTS1 model/environment pair, and admits
that selected pair before sealing. Missing model or environment members,
incompatible environment generation and changed independent context cannot
produce a selected release. The paired borrow and eviction protection remain
in force through sealing. Readiness times remain cumulative, from the same
original operation origin; no partial stage resets the clock or resource grant.

The [native grouped contract](CELL_NATIVE_SHARD_RESEARCH_V2.md) retains original
indivisible M12 parent claims and independent CGJ3 child identities. Original

## Discovery observations and qualification

The original `higher_sequence_never_masks_an_already_observed_equivocation`
test keeps its original request timeout, overall deadline, connection/byte/
signature budgets, final output refusal and zero-provider-dial assertion.

The same consumer fetch path now retains its discovery observation even when
the later fetch fails. Tests can inspect completed replies, record validation,
bootstrap rejection, replay/conflict rejection and provider attempts. Server
accept/write counters alone do not prove that the client received a complete
frame or authenticated its contents.

Real TCP negative controls distinguish a complete signed A1/C2/B1 conflict
from a first or conflicting reply truncated before its final byte, and from a
complete reply with an invalid signature. Only authenticated, fully received
statements can establish the conflict precondition. Truncated or unauthenticated
data must neither fabricate a conflict nor refund consumed network allowances.
These controls explain observable stages; they do not retroactively identify
the cause of the historical hosted failure without its missing observations.

## Persistent CURRENT belongs to the original Hepta owner

M09 supplies verified bytes. It does not create another CURRENT database,
operation journal, signer, selection authority or FinalUse issuer. Existing
process-local tickets are never persisted or reused after reopening a pool.

The required adapter must bind the original operation and owner scope, exact
predecessor generation/writer epoch, canonical target CellBackendManifestV2 and
independent signed selection to the complete CTS1/TCV1/TCV2/CHB1 identities.
Chain and Hepta digest domains retain their exact meanings. Network `latest`,
a truncated SHA-512, or a newly hashed DTO is not a signed selection.

Only the original Version/CURRENT host may revalidate Artifact and withdrawals,
prepare the next generation, consume the original FinalUse grant and commit
its existing durable CAS. Running tasks retain their original generation.
Lost acknowledgements and uncertain effects reconcile the original operation;
they cannot mint a new nonce or issue another initial effect. Cold startup opens
the original CURRENT owner, recovers its operation and then reconstructs and
verifies that owner's selected model/environment bytes. Returning to earlier
content is a new generation, never a rollback of local authority history.

The retained Version client/host in Hepta PR #5981 provides real original-owner
mechanisms for strict TrainingNoUpdate. It does not by itself implement this
general BT-release adapter or qualify ordinary Adopt. Closing this boundary
requires actual ordinary A/publish/C use and daemon plus Root cold recovery on
the same source pair, with exact model/environment generation readback and
withdrawal, missing-dependency, ABI, disk-full, process-interruption and ABA
negative cases.
