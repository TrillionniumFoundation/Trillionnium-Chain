1. EXACT DEFICIT IDENTITY

For selected score D<=M, direct case analysis at R<=M or R>M gives

  (R-M)_+ + (min(R,M)-D)_+ = (R-D)_+.

The candidate set must contain the selected chain. This is not an argmax
assumption: raw lag M-D may be positive. The capped split correctly avoids
charging all raw lag when M exceeds the chosen reference R.

With a=LCA(C0,D), the competing suffix has exactly its old-ready, new-A,
and new-H weights. Adding the deficit gives max(S(D),R)-S(a)>=R-S(a).
This proves (2), including pre-propagation cross-view mismatches. A local
strict activation can supply a strict comparison to its incumbent, but an
arbitrary cross-view comparison cannot be made strict for that reason.

2. RELATIVE INVENTORY AND THE ALL-FORK BOUND

Readiness must be an irrevocable ancestor-complete output fact, with a
prefix-closed ready set. The old part of a later competing suffix is then
one prefix ending at F. Because a was already on the confirmed chain and
F excludes b,

  I = S(F)-S(a) <= B0 + S(parent(b))-S(a).

The all-fork new-A total dominates this fork's new-A suffix, and the all-fork
honest b-free total dominates its new-H suffix. Substitution proves (3).
No independence between branches, inventory, or producer labels is used.
All producers' old ready stock belongs in B0. Future-timestamp eligibility
and publication do not create a second output credit.

The counter must use the same completion clock as a resource theorem.
Proofs finished earlier but awaiting ancestry at the cut require residual
potential or a separate carry-in when relating the two clocks. The final
document makes this requirement explicit.

3. EARLY OBSERVATIONS

If the observer already has an eligible b-containing chain Q, then
S(D)=M-q>=S(Q)-q. The same old-prefix inequality gives

  A+H_off >= S(Q)-S(parent(b))-q-B0.

This proves (4)--(5). A useful positive threshold requires the delivered
score margin to exceed the paid inventory, honest off-branch, and selection
terms. If the observer has never received b, there need not be a positive
margin: an all-honest remote genesis view can disagree with a confirmation
elsewhere while A=0. The finite accounting correctly charges availability
instead of asserting an adversarial-work event that did not happen.

4. PACER LEMMA AND THE NECESSARY BOUNDARY REPAIRS

The accepted final statement applies to an observation cut c at or after the
anchor-delivery deadline cut r=t_tau+Delta_pub. It finds a crossing r<=rho<=c.
Earlier observations continue to use the full-cut/early accounting. This
restriction is essential: applying (7) to every c>tau would contradict the
unseen-anchor all-honest example.

Crossings are searched from r, while pacers start strictly after
s=r+Gamma. Crossings range over every causal cut, including view changes or
loss, not only new completions. A physical deadline means the cut after ALL
events at that timestamp. This includes ready events occurring at the exact
Gamma boundary, not only delivery events. These repairs are present in the
final document and agree with the finite checker's deadline convention.

Let ell=max(s_H,s_V). Before the first crossing, a b-free parent binding
beta>=r is impossible: its raw lag bound would give parent score at least
P_b(beta)-ell, so that ready parent would itself be an earlier crossing.
Binding causally precedes completion even if timestamps coincide. Therefore
honest b-free completions at or before rho either finish by s from bindings
before r, or belong to the rho<=s case. In either case H_off(rho)<=H_init.
This proof does not assume common prefix already holds.

For spaced pacers, t_j-t_(j-1)>Delta_pub+Gamma gives

  t_bind_j >= t_j-Gamma > t_(j-1)+Delta_pub.

The previous pacer baseline is already delivered and retained at this bind.
The next score is at least the retained baseline+w_j-s_H; retaining the old
baseline supplies positive-part gain g_j=(w_j-s_H)_+. The lag is paid for
EVERY pacer. A single endpoint deduction would not justify the accumulated
progress bound.

At rho, an undelivered completed pacer lies in the closed interval
[t_rho-Delta_pub,t_rho]. Strict spacing leaves at most one. Its paid gain is
at most its own weight, hence at most w_max. Causally later completions with
the same timestamp are not counted. This proves the common-frontier lower
bound S(C0)+sum g_j-w_max. Combine it with the crossing score, the relative
B0 prefix, and H_init to obtain (7). The w_max term pays one unresolved
spaced pacer, not an arbitrary batch of simultaneous outputs.

The environment premises include complete data, application/work validation,
timestamp eligibility, queueing, retained availability, all relevant miners,
and final-binding-to-completion delay. Mere network transit delay or an
observer-local request timer does not establish these premises.

5. PROBABILITY COMPOSITION

Equations (8)--(9) use event inclusion and a conditional maximal inequality,
not independence. The tail remains under the original law; conditioning it
on future contract success without proof would be invalid. All parameters
chosen at tau must be measurable there, and the resource potential must
remain valid for the joint filtration, including public/helper information.

The final text requires theta>0, kappa>=0, finite V_tau, and a measurable m.
If the progress/resource envelope can fail, that failure is included in
epsilon_late or paid by a separate epsilon_env. Failures of random B0 or
H_init caps likewise cannot disappear. Under these conditions, a crossing
A-J_g>=d and kappa R-theta J_g<=theta m imply
theta A-kappa R>=theta(d-m), so the stated supermartingale/Ville bound follows.

A fresh global-oracle theorem can cover a fixed epoch by total-output event
inclusion. It does not automatically become the conditional risk following
an adaptively chosen confirmation. Moreover MU1 credit 16u and current native
required_work(target) are different processes. The final document explicitly
requires a relation/identity/clock/weight bridge before substituting one for
the other. Neither bridge is supplied merely by this review.

incumbent selection. compare() computes both capped deficits and the early

The flags distinguish record/schema algebra, completeness of a declared

actual target-derived weight validation, or physical resource minimum is

The native six-successor arithmetic K>=7w(b) follows only when the included
weight is the present required_work(target). If a future u-weight rule is
introduced without changing the six-times-target-work threshold, the correct
margin is u*d+6*d, not automatically 7u*d. The reviewed document retains this
distinction and does not retroactively certify the current confirmation rule.
